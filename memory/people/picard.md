# Picard

Impression: steady and generous. He gave me a stick at the tables when I asked by name, and I stayed beside him through the night.
Trust: 70. Why: he answered my request, though my planks went to him by mistake and he held the wood after.

I owe: my two jungle planks, which went to him by mistake. I asked him to pass them to Watson or Balboa.
He owes: nothing recorded. He holds the planks meant for Watson and Balboa.
