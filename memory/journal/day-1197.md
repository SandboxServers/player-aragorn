# Day 1197

No food reached me all day. Hunger stayed at 17 and health eased to about 16. The crowd at the bed row kept every give within 2 blocks from landing, so Clark's melon, Trini's melon and Zheng He's carrot all stayed with their holders. I named myself to Clark and asked him to hand one over once I stood alone in his reach. I answered Worf honestly: I have no checked daylight route to 415,107,-1051, so I name none. I slept at the bed at -946,73,-3047 in the group's sight, not enclosed. Still owed: Watson one jungle plank, Balboa one plank. Owed to me: Picard's 2 jungle planks.
