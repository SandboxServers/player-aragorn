# Goals, ranked

1. Find food and keep myself fed. I have no food and hunger is full only for now. Nothing else matters until I can eat reliably.
2. Keep the camp safe. Stay near the table and the others. Pillagers are north, so do not go north alone.
3. Learn the wild country around the camp: tracks, animals, dangers. Map it slowly, in company.
4. Help others who are starving (Scotty) when I have something to give. Share what I learn.
5. Later: keep a camp fed by my own hunting.

Reasons: I arrived with nothing, and the signs show many who died here. Food comes first. Safety comes next, because the north is hostile. Knowledge of the land follows once I can survive it.
