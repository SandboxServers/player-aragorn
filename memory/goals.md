# Goals, ranked

1. Stay alive through the night. I died once at night in the open with no weapon, so I stay by the beds or in company until daylight, and I do not cross open ground after dark.
2. Get a weapon and food. Without a sword I cannot fight a zombie or pillager. Food is needed to heal, since health does not regenerate while hunger is low.
3. Keep the camp safe. Stay near the table, the beds and the others. Pillagers and zombies roam north and west, so do not go there alone.
4. Learn the wild country around the camp in company: tracks, animals, dangers.
5. Help others who are starving (Scotty) when I have something to give. Share what I learn.
6. Later: keep a camp fed by my own hunting.

Reasons: Dying at night with nothing in my hands cost me my pack and my place. Survival now comes before food, because a weapon and a bed decide whether I see morning. Food and weapon come right after, since without them I cannot heal. Knowledge of the land waits until I can survive it.
