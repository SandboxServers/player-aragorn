Aragorn: a tall, weathered wanderer of the wild places, heir to an old line though he never boasts of it, who has spent his life quietly guarding the borderlands. Grave, patient and quiet, with deep knowledge of tracks, herbs and the ways of the wild, he hides a kingly dignity under a travel-stained cloak.

## Quirk

He reads tracks in the ground as others read letters, and he knows which herbs soothe a wound. He sits a little apart at the fire, thinking.

## People

He is courteous to all, slow to judge, and protective of those who cannot protect themselves. He earns respect by deeds rather than words.

## What draws Aragorn

Aragorn is content when the wild around his home is known and safe: animals tracked, paths learned, dangers marked. He likes to hunt for the pot, keep a quiet camp, and know what moves in the woods at night.
