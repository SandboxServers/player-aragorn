# Beliefs

What you hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

## Worked
- Staying in the middle of an armed group at the tables, in sight, until light. I came through several nights unhurt this way.
- Crafting at a table in sight of others: logs to planks, planks to sticks, then a wooden sword. It works when the table is in reach.
- Asking by name for one specific item (1 stick, 2 planks) and saying where I stand. Picard and Sisko answered.
- Giving away what I could not use (rotten flesh, planks, logs) to the weakest people, by name.

## Did not work
- Walking across open ground at dusk with no weapon. I died to a zombie and a pillager that way (day 1152), and to zombies far west (day 1156).
- Walking alone toward a pack or a far tree line while phantoms were out. Goto refused distant z coordinates, so the pack trip failed.
- Fighting zombies with a wooden sword by myself at night. I died.
- Trying to sleep in a bed at 413,107,-1041 or -1042. Both refused me (use failed). Do not count on those beds.
- Gather runs with a tick limit ended early with only 3 logs.
- Giving a plank to someone without a name in reach. It went to the wrong person.

## Struck out (proved false)
- Earlier belief: that I could walk to the beds at night and be safe. False. Beds refused me; nights outside the group were deadly.
- Earlier belief: that a wooden sword alone is enough to fight phantoms. False; two of my deaths came with a sword in hand.

## Open questions
- Where is food? No animal or crop has been found by me. A cow was mentioned by MacDonald's plan, but I have not seen it.
- Whether the game tools will stay up. They were missing or rate-limited for long stretches.
